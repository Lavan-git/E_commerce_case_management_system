# Delivery Policy

**Document type:** Policy  
**Domain:** Delivery disputes  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

This policy defines how cases involving delayed, failed, or allegedly missing deliveries should be investigated.

## Order not delivered

When a customer reports that an order was not delivered:

1. verify the relevant order
2. check the delivery record
3. inspect delivery attempts where available
4. determine whether the shipment is delayed, failed, delivered, or otherwise unresolved
5. record the evidence used to determine the case status

## Delivery attempts

A delivery attempt should not automatically be treated as successful delivery.

The status of an attempt should be evaluated together with the overall delivery state.

## Delivered but customer disputes receipt

When the delivery system shows a completed delivery but the customer disputes receipt, the case should remain open for investigation and should not be resolved solely from the customer's statement or the delivery status in isolation.

Available delivery evidence should be reviewed.

## Delayed delivery

A delayed delivery should remain distinguishable from a failed delivery.

The case should be updated as new delivery information becomes available.

## Resolution

A delivery case may be resolved when sufficient evidence establishes the operational outcome and the required customer-facing action has been completed or initiated.
