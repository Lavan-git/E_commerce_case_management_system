# Vendor Payout Policy

**Document type:** Policy  
**Domain:** Vendor payouts  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

This policy defines how vendor cases involving missing, delayed, or unexpected payouts should be investigated.

## Payout discrepancy

When a vendor reports that a payout amount is lower than expected:

1. identify the relevant vendor payout
2. verify the payout amount
3. inspect the underlying eligible transactions or order-level records
4. determine whether deductions or adjustments explain the difference
5. record the evidence supporting the resolution

## Delayed payout

A payout reported as delayed should be checked against its recorded payout state and relevant processing information.

A payout should not be described as completed until the system confirms the completed state.

## Vendor dashboard issue

When a vendor reports incorrect payout information in the dashboard, the case should distinguish between:

- an underlying payout-data issue
- a display or synchronization issue
- an issue requiring further investigation

## Resolution

Vendor payout cases should preserve the relevant payout identifier, vendor identifier, reported issue, verified financial state, and resolution outcome.

Financial amounts should be derived from authoritative records rather than re-entered manually when the underlying records are available.
